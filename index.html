Yuguang

Notes on AI, agents, and building with them.

Hi — I'm Yuguang. I am an applied AI engineer and scientist building, shipping, and evaluating AI and Agentic systems. In this Github Pages, I want to document observation, understanding, lessons learned, and thoughts about the rapidly changing AI space. I want to achieve two goals as part of my writing: 1. putting thoughts into knowledge, turning a vague feeling into something concrete, and internalize it as the process. 2. Share to the public, help people to understand, and welcome feedbacks so that we all learn. To achieve these two goals, I will only share knowledge from my own experiences and original thoughts, while not relying on AI to write any articles. I will only use AI to correct grammar and make my sentences concise whenever possible. I welcome you to create github issues or DM me to provide feedbacks. Thank you!
